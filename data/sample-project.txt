Parkview Apartments is a six-story, 120-unit multifamily project in Kansas City, Missouri. The project includes cast-in-place concrete foundations, structural steel, TPO roofing, waterproofing, metal framing and drywall, painting, LVT and tile flooring, plumbing, HVAC, fire protection, electrical distribution, lighting, fire alarm and low-voltage systems. Estimated construction value is $18,000,000. Bids are due November 20, 2026.
